**The site for the seventh Bulgarian Community Cup**, a 2v2 osu! tournament.
Sign-ups to grand finals, the bracket, the stream overlay and a tray app for refs and streamers.

## The stream

The overlay is a page on the site, added to OBS as a browser source over the tourney client. It follows the streamer's current match and switches scenes on its own: intro while the teams roll, the pool while they pick, gameplay when the map starts, scores after it ends.
